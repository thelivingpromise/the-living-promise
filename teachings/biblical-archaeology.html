Biblical Archaeology

Biblical archaeology studies places, objects, buildings, inscriptions, and other material remains from the regions and periods connected with the Bible. Excavations and discoveries can reveal details about everyday life, trade, architecture, political power, writing, and religious practices in the ancient world.

Archaeological evidence needs to be interpreted carefully. A discovery may illuminate the setting of a passage without proving every detail of a particular interpretation. By considering archaeological findings alongside historical sources and Scripture, learners can gain a richer picture of the world in which biblical events took place.
